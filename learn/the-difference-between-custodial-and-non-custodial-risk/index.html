The difference between custodial and non-custodial risk

September 19, 2026

Custodial wallets (exchanges, some apps) mean a third party holds your private keys — convenient, but you're exposed to their solvency and security. Non-custodial wallets mean you hold the keys yourself — you remove counterparty risk, but you also remove any safety net if a key is lost or a device is compromised.
