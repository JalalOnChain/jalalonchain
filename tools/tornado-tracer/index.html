Tornado Cash Timing Tracer

Search an Ethereum address that deposited into Tornado Cash, and this tool scans the same pool's activity around that time to surface withdrawal addresses whose timing lines up closely enough to be a candidate match. These are statistical links from public on-chain data, not proof of common ownership — read the methodology below before relying on anything here.

Search

Looks only at Ethereum mainnet pools. Funds can sit in a Tornado Cash pool indefinitely before withdrawal — a wider window catches more candidates but also more noise.
Methodology & limitations

What this does. Tornado Cash deposits are plain, unrelayed transactions — the depositing address is public. Withdrawals are usually sent through a relayer so the gas-paying address isn't the recipient, but the recipient address is still a public parameter inside the withdrawal transaction. For each deposit your searched address made, this tool pulls every withdrawal from that same fixed-denomination pool inside your selected time window, decodes each one's recipient, and ranks them by how soon after the deposit they happened.

Why timing is a real signal, not a guess. Tornado Cash's own contracts enforce no minimum or maximum holding period — a deposit's commitment just sits in the pool's Merkle tree until someone presents a valid proof to withdraw it, which can happen minutes or years later. Most real users don't wait long, so a withdrawal that lands shortly after your deposit — especially when few other withdrawals happened in that same window — is a meaningfully stronger candidate than one separated by weeks with dozens of other withdrawals in between. This tool shows you that context (the gap, and how many other withdrawals occurred in the same window) rather than inventing a false-precision "confidence score."

  • Direction matters. This only works deposit-first: search an address that sent a deposit, and it surfaces candidate recipients. It cannot currently work backwards from a bare recipient address, because a relayed withdrawal leaves no trace in the recipient's own transaction history — finding it requires already knowing which pool and rough time window to scan.
  • A short gap is a hint, not a verdict. Other correlating signals investigators use — identical split-denomination deposit sequences, matching gas-fee fingerprints, re-used addresses downstream — are not computed here. Treat matches as leads to corroborate, not conclusions.
  • Ethereum mainnet only, covering the ETH, DAI, cDAI, USDC, USDT and WBTC fixed-denomination pools. Tornado Cash's L2/sidechain deployments (Arbitrum, Optimism, BSC, Polygon, Avalanche, xDAI) aren't covered yet.
  • Legal status. Tornado Cash was removed from OFAC's Specially Designated Nationals list in 2025, after the Fifth Circuit ruled the smart contracts aren't "property" the agency had authority to sanction. Using or analyzing it is not, on its own, prohibited — but compliance obligations depend on your own jurisdiction and policies.
