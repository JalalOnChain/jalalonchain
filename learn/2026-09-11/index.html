Stablecoins aren't all backed the same way

September 11, 2026

Fiat-collateralized stablecoins (like USDC, USDT) hold reserves off-chain in cash and short-term securities; crypto-collateralized ones (like DAI) are over-collateralized by on-chain assets to absorb price swings; algorithmic designs try to hold a peg through incentives alone with no hard collateral — the last category has the worst historical track record for depegging under stress.
